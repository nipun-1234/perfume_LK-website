Art of Ancient Distillation

From Ceylon Soil to Crystal Flacon

Every drop is slow-distilled over months using 400-year-old traditional copper alembics.

01

Wild Forest Foraging

Ethically wild-harvested agarwood from the mist-covered mountain valleys of Adam's Peak and Nilgala reserves.

02

Copper Steam Alembic

Fractional steam distillation in handcrafted copper vessels in Kandy, capturing pure volatile botanical oils.

03

32% Extrait Aging

Matured for 180 days in French oak casks with 24K gold foil infusion to achieve magnetic sillage and 16+ hour longevity.

04

Crystal Laser Engraving

Hand-filled into beveled crystal decanters with precious caps and individually laser-engraved with your monogram in Colombo.
